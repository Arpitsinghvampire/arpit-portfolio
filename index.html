01 / ABOUT

Curiosity-led engineering.
Purpose-driven work.

My journey started with computer vision and deep learning, where I built and experimented with neural network architectures for image understanding. Since then, I’ve expanded into machine learning workflows, LLM-powered applications, recommendation systems, and full-stack tools that make intelligent systems useful beyond a notebook.

I’m currently pursuing a Master’s in Artificial Intelligence at IIT Hyderabad, with research interests around deep brain stimulation (DBS). I’m especially interested in how data-driven methods can support better scientific understanding and practical decisions.

I enjoy taking a problem from first principles — understanding the data, building a model, evaluating its behaviour, and thinking about how it could be used in the real world.
